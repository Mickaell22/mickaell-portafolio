## Context

A system built for a client to manage their cell phone repair shop operations. The business needed to track repair orders, spare parts inventory, assigned technicians and invoicing in one place.

## Features

- **Repair orders:** creation, status tracking, history per device
- **Parts:** inventory with low-stock alerts
- **Technicians:** assignment to orders, workload per technician
- **Invoicing:** invoice generation when closing an order
- **Users and roles:** administrator and technician with differentiated views

## Tech stack

**Backend:** Node.js with Express. PostgreSQL with a relational structure for orders, parts and technicians. JWT for authentication and role control.

**Frontend:** React SPA with role-protected routes.
